## Step 3: Cash Flow Forecasting

Revenue on paper is not cash in your account. Cash flow timing is what actually keeps a business alive.

**Monthly cash flow forecast (do this 3 months ahead):**

```

==================
                        Month 1    Month 2    Month 3
Starting Cash:          $________  $________  $________
+ Revenue In:           $________  $________  $________
- Expenses Out:         $________  $________  $________
= Ending Cash:          $________  $________  $________
```

**Cash flow timing rules:**
- Revenue often comes in AFTER the work is 已完成 (invoices have Net-15 or Net-30 terms). Budget for this lag.
- Some expenses are lumpy (annual subscriptions, quarterly contractor payments). Spread these into monthly equivalents in your budget so you're not surprised.
- Keep a cash reserve of 2-3 months of expenses. This is your runway 缓冲区. Without it, one bad month 可以 threaten the business.

**Cash flow danger signals:**
- Ending cash drops below 1 month of expenses → urgent. 剪切 spending or accelerate collections immediately.
- Revenue is growing but cash is flat → you're spending everything you earn. Examine 变量 expenses.
- Revenue is lumpy (big months, dead months) → smooth it out with recurring revenue models or 构建 a larger cash reserve.

## Step 5: 跟踪 Monthly (The 15-Minute Review)

At the end of every month, spend 15 minutes on this review:

**Tools for tracking:** A shared Google Sheet is sufficient for most solopreneurs. Dedicated tools (QuickBooks, FreshBooks, Wave) 添加 值 once revenue exceeds $5K/month or you have complex expenses. Wave is free and handles basic bookkeeping well.

## Step 6: Tax Planning (Integrated, Not Afterthought)

Tax is an expense like any other. Budget for it monthly — not just once a year in a panic.

**Solopreneur tax budget rule:** 集合 aside 25-30% of every revenue payment into a separate "tax savings" account. This covers:
- Self-employment tax (Social Security + Medicare)
- Federal and state income tax
- Quarterly estimated tax payments (due Jan 15, Apr 15, Jun 15, Sep 15 in the US)

**If you're outside the US:** Tax rules vary enormously by country. The percentage 可以 differ but the principle is the same — 集合 aside a fixed percentage of revenue immediately, before you spend it.

**If you haven't been doing this and owe back taxes:** Calculate the total owed, divide by the months until the deadline, and 集合 that aside each month. Do not ignore it.

## Financial Planning Mistakes 迁移到 Avoid
- Treating revenue as profit. Revenue minus expenses = profit. Many solopreneurs conflate the two.
- Not paying yourself a salary. If you don't pay yourself, you don't know if the business is actually profitable for YOU.
- Ignoring taxes until April (or your country's equivalent). Tax surprises are the #1 financial crisis for solopreneurs.
- Budgeting optimistically. Budget conservatively on revenue (assume less), aggressively on expenses (assume more). Positive surprises are much better than negative ones.
- Never revisiting the budget. A budget 集合 in January is stale by March. 更新 monthly.
