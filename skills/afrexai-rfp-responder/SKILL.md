# RFP & Proposal Responder

Turn RFPs, RFIs, and proposal requests into winning responses. Fast.

## 功能

Takes an RFP document (or 键 环境要求) and generates a structured proposal 响应 with:

- **Executive 摘要** — why you're the right fit, in 3 sentences
- **环境要求 矩阵** — every requirement mapped 迁移到 your capability + evidence
- **定价 截面** — structured 选项 (good/better/best) with 清空 deliverables
- **Risk 缓解** — 主动 answers 迁移到 "what 可能 go wrong"
- **Timeline** — realistic milestones with 依赖 called out
- **Differentiators** — what makes your bid stand out vs. generic competitors

## 用法

粘贴 the RFP text or 键 环境要求, then tell the 代理:

```
Here's the RFP: [paste text or attach file]

Our company: [brief description]
Our strengths: [2-3 key differentiators]
Budget range: [if known]
```

The 代理 将 produce a 完成 draft 响应 ready for 审查 and submission.

## 提示

- Include your past win 示例 — the 代理 weaves them into proof points
- Specify word/page limits if the RFP has them
- Ask for a "合规 checklist" first if the RFP is complex
- Use "score my 响应" 迁移到 获取 a self-assessment before submitting

## 适用人群

- Agencies responding 迁移到 government or enterprise RFPs
- B2B 服务 companies competing for contracts
- Consultancies that burn 20+ hours per proposal
