# 定价 策略 Analyzer

分析并优化 定价 for any product or 服务. Covers value-based, cccost-pluscompetitive, and tiered 定价 models.

## 功能
- Runs 定价 power analysis (1% 价格 increase = 12.5% profit increase for most businesses)
- Calculates willingness-to-pay ranges using Van Westendorp 方法论
- Models tier structures (Good/Better/Best) with anchor 定价
- Identifies 定价 mistakes: leaving money on 表, racing 迁移到 bottom, ignoring segments
- Generates competitor 定价 matrices
- Produces actionable 定价 recommendations with revenue impact estimates

## 用法
Ask your 代理: "分析 定价 for [product/service]" or "Help me 集合 定价 for [new offering]"

Provide:
- Current 定价 (if any)
- Target customer segment
- 键 competitors and their 定价
- 成本 structure (fixed + 变量)
- Current 转换 rate and 卷

The 代理 将 返回 a full 定价 analysis with specific recommendations and projected revenue impact.

## 定价 Frameworks Included

### 值-Based 定价
Calculate 价格 based on quantified customer 值, not your costs. The #1 定价 mistake is anchoring 迁移到 成本 instead of 值 delivered.

### Tiered 定价 (Good/Better/Best)
- **Good**: Entry point, captures price-sensitive segment (60-70% of 值)
- **Better**: Sweet spot, where most revenue comes from (85-90% of 值)  
- **Best**: Premium anchor that makes Better look reasonable (100%+ of 值)

### Competitive Positioning
- Premium (20-50% above market): requires 清空 differentiation
- Parity (±10%): compete on features/experience
- Penetration (20-40% below): 卷 play, requires 成本 advantage

### 价格 弹性 Quick 测试
- Drop 价格 10%, measure 卷 更改
- If 卷 increases <10% → you have 定价 power, raise prices
- If 卷 increases >15% → you're in 弹性 territory, 优化 for 卷

## 键 指标 迁移到 跟踪
- **ACV** (Annual Contract 值) — are deals getting bigger or smaller?
- **Discount frequency** — if >30% of deals 需要 discounts, your 列表 价格 is wrong
- **时间-to-close by 价格 point** — longer cycles at higher prices 可以 still be more profitable
- **Expansion revenue %** — best signal that your 定价 captures ongoing 值
